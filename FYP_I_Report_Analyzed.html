ABSTRACT

The AI-Powered Recruitment Platform is a comprehensive web-based application that revolutionizes the recruitment process through artificial intelligence integration. The platform addresses critical limitations of traditional recruitment systems by automating CV analysis, providing intelligent job-candidate matching, and enabling both voice and video interview capabilities.

The system implements a multi-role architecture supporting three user types: Candidates, Recruiters, and Administrators. Candidates can upload CVs for AI-powered skill analysis, browse job opportunities, apply for positions, participate in voice/video interviews, and receive career guidance. Recruiters can post jobs, review AI-analyzed applications, conduct interviews, and manage the recruitment workflow. Administrators oversee platform analytics, user management, and system performance.

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Project Overview

The AI-Powered Recruitment Platform represents a paradigm shift in recruitment technology by integrating cutting-edge artificial intelligence with modern web development practices. The platform serves as a comprehensive solution that addresses the entire recruitment lifecycle from job posting to candidate selection.

The system implements a sophisticated multi-role architecture with distinct interfaces and functionalities for candidates, recruiters, and administrators. The platform leverages Google Gemini AI for intelligent CV analysis, providing compatibility scoring and skill matching that significantly reduces manual screening efforts.

Traditional recruitment processes suffer from several critical inefficiencies:

Manual Screening Challenges:

  • Time-intensive CV review processes requiring hours of manual analysis
  • Subjective evaluation leading to biased candidate selection
  • Limited scalability for high-volume recruitment campaigns
  • Difficulty in matching candidate skills with job requirements accurately

Communication Barriers:

  • Lack of real-time interaction between candidates and recruiters
  • Delayed feedback loops in the application process
  • Geographical constraints limiting interview accessibility
  • Manual scheduling and coordination overhead

Technology Gaps:

  • Absence of intelligent matching algorithms
  • Limited analytics and reporting capabilities
  • Poor user experience on existing platforms
  • Lack of integrated communication tools
